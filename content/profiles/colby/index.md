## *Machine Check*

**Engine:** Stock B6ze with Racing Beat intake, Koyorad radiator and Borla exhaust.

**Drivetrain:** Stock 5 speed and 4.3 welded diff.

**Footwork:** Rev9 coilovers, Destroy Or Die cut knuckles with oem arms all the around.

**Aero:** Wings West type 2 front and rear bumper cover with d max style hood and a singular Jet Stream TSI.

**Wheels:** Ssr XR4 14x7+0 165/65 with a 15mm adapter.

**Interior:** Complete OEM interior with a dash mat, Thrash Racing 330mm streering wheel with a matching Thrash shifter, Sparco sprint bucket seat with stock passenger seat.
